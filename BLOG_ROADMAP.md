# IBE Knowledge Center — Year-One Blog Roadmap

**Audience:** Bakery owners, GMs, and operations decision-makers
**Tone:** Practical, decision-oriented, ROI-aware (no academic framing)
**Cadence:** 1 post per week × 52 weeks
**Structure:** Knowledge Center → 5 Classes → 14 Units → 52 Chapters (one chapter = one blog)

---

## How each blog is structured

Every post follows the same template so the section feels cohesive:

1. **Hook** — a problem the GM is actually facing (lost shifts, failed audits, capex pressure)
2. **The decision framework** — practical guidance, not product pitch
3. **Branded tips** — 2–4 inline pull-quotes linking to relevant IBE products / contact / quote cart
4. **Sources** — citations at the end (industry pubs, regulatory bodies, OEM literature)
5. **Sticky table of contents** — scrolls with the reader, highlights current section

## Branded tip pattern (used throughout posts)

Inline boxes styled in IBE orange (#C05621) with the chrome-italic accent. Three flavors:

> **→ Spec Tip:** Link to a specific product page or PDF spec sheet.
> **→ Quote Tip:** Link to the contact form or quote cart with a pre-filled product context.
> **→ Reference Tip:** Link to another blog post in the Knowledge Center for deeper reading.

## Sources policy

Every post ends with a "Sources & Further Reading" section. Acceptable sources:

- Regulatory bodies (FDA, USDA, BISSC, NSF)
- Industry publications (Bakery Business, Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery, Food Engineering)
- Trade associations (American Bakers Association, Retail Bakers of America)
- OEM literature (when factual/non-promotional)
- Academic research (peer-reviewed where possible)

No anonymous blogs, no AI-generated content as primary sources, no competitor product pages.
